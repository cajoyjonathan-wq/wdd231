The Pearl of the Orient

Located on the Southeast Asia, The Philippine Island is rich in natural beauty, tropical and flavorful cuisines, and hospitable, resilient people. This country is being colonized by the Spaniards, Americans, and Japanese...and over the course of time, local and foreign taste have merged together to create the finest dishes of the Country.

A taste that connects the Past and Present

Food is a universal language that connects all people together. Some pre-colonial Filipino Dishes are still being enjoyed on Special Occasions in the country today. Filipinos are passionate and creative in their own way of cooking. Using local ingredients, and sometimes indigenous ways of cooking, Filipinos have made foods that brings a ton of flavor.

A Legacy of Resilience

Alongside the resilence of enduring storms, typhoons, earthquakes, and civil unrest, the Filipino Spirit still finds its way to be creative and passionate. Like the resilency of the people, the flavors of the Dishes of every Filipino Cuisine is a testament of the Filipino's unwavering resolve to never give up.
